Habits are easier to keep when they feel like a game.

## Features

- Daily quests with XP rewards
- Streaks shown as an XP bar
- Works offline as an installable PWA

<Tip variant="emerald" title="Design note">
  Rewards are small and frequent. A full level-up takes about a week of consistency.
</Tip>
