A UI concept for a shop that sells everything you need for a trip to the Nether.

## Highlights

- Instant filters with no page reloads
- A checkout you can complete with only a keyboard
- Every colour pair meets WCAG AA contrast

## What I'd change

The product grid loads every image at once. Next time I'd lazy-load images below the fold.
