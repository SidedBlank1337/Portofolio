Our server needed a better way to handle whitelist requests than "DM whoever is online".

## What it does

- `/whitelist` sends a request that admins approve with one button
- `/online` lists who is playing right now
- Posts a daily build challenge at 6 pm

<Tip variant="info" title="Lesson learned">
  Rate limits are real. Cache server status for 30 seconds instead of polling on every command.
</Tip>
