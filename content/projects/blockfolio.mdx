This site is itself a project. I wanted a portfolio that felt like booting up a game instead of reading a CV.

## Goals

- Feel like Minecraft without becoming hard to use
- Static, fast, and deployable to Vercel with no backend
- Accessible: keyboard navigation, focus states and reduced-motion support

<Tip variant="diamond" title="Try it">
  Press 1–5 to jump between pages, E to open your inventory, and hunt for the hidden achievements.
</Tip>

## How it's built

- **Next.js App Router** with every page pre-rendered at build time
- **MDX** for project write-ups like this one
- **CSS Modules** with design tokens for the three dimension themes
- The game's own textures and GUI sprites, scaled with hard pixel edges

## What I learned

Pixel art and modern layout fight each other. Most of the work was making texture-heavy panels stay readable at every screen size.
